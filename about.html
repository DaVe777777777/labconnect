ABOUT LABCONNECT

Accessible and flexible laboratory learning for Grade 10.

What is LabConnect?

LabConnect is a web-based Science Laboratory Learning Management System developed to support accessible and flexible laboratory learning among Grade 10 learners.

The platform provides a centralized space where teachers can share experiment demonstration videos, lesson handouts, laboratory instructions, activity sheets, quizzes, and announcements.

It is designed to help learners continue developing their scientific knowledge and skills even when conducting laboratory experiments in a physical classroom is not always possible.

Why LabConnect?

Science is best learned not only by reading concepts but also by observing, investigating, and asking questions. However, some schools may experience limitations in laboratory equipment, materials, scheduling, or access to safe laboratory facilities.

LabConnect provides another way to engage with laboratory learning through video demonstrations, guided observations, and teacher-approved hands-on activities.

How to Use LabConnect

  1. Choose the weekly lesson you need to study.
  2. Read the learning objectives and lesson discussion.
  3. Download the lesson handout for additional reading.
  4. Watch the experiment demonstration video.
  5. Read the experiment instructions and safety reminders.
  6. Perform the experiment only if materials are available and your teacher approves. Otherwise, complete the video observation activity.
  7. Answer the guide questions and submit your activity.
  8. Take the weekly quiz.
  9. Check announcements for reminders and deadlines.
