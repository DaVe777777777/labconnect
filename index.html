Welcome to LabConnect!

Welcome to LabConnect, an online learning platform designed to make Science laboratory learning more accessible and flexible.

Here, you can explore different Chemistry topics through lesson discussions, experiment demonstration videos, downloadable handouts, and interactive learning activities.

You do not always need to be inside a physical laboratory to learn Science. Through video demonstrations and guided activities, you can observe scientific processes, record findings, answer questions, and connect what you learn to everyday experiences.

What can you do on LabConnect?

  • Watch experiment demonstration videos.
  • Download weekly lesson handouts in Word format.
  • Read laboratory instructions and safety reminders.
  • Complete activities and submit your answers online.
  • Take weekly quizzes to check your understanding.
  • Read important announcements from your teacher.
